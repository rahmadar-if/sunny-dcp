# Sunny CRM

Sunny CRM is a unified customer relationship management platform built on Laravel. It brings together the full sales pipeline and multi-channel marketing capabilities under one system — giving teams a single view of the customer from first touch to closed deal and beyond.

Built for organizations that need Sales and Marketing to work from the same data, the same workflows, and the same platform.

---

## What's Included

Everything in **Sunny Sales** and **Sunny Marketing**, unified:

### Sales
- Lead management and pipeline tracking
- Opportunity management with quotation history
- Order, invoice, and contract management
- Sales dashboard with pipeline and revenue metrics

### Marketing
- Email and WhatsApp blast campaigns
- Event management with audience segmentation
- Campaign delivery tracking and performance reporting

### Unified Customer View
- Shared account, contact, and lead data across Sales and Marketing
- Complete customer history across all interactions — activities, campaigns, deals, and documents
- Consistent access control and menu management across the full platform
